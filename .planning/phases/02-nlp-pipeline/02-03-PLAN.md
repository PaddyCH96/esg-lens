    the match key IS the severity, and a second matcher for the negation phrases. Add patterns with

    `negation_guard.window` from the lexicon. Demotion is exactly one tier, floored at 0.

    `doc[s:e][0].pos_ == "ADJ"`. Dropping `fine` from the lexicon does not remove the ambiguity,

    that reasoning in a comment.

    is by construction mostly boilerplate risk language — reproduces that failure one layer down.
    Terms are the UNION across sentences, not just those from the max-severity sentence: NLP-06 says
    the terms are the audit trail, and an auditor shown only `['oil spill']` for a section that also
    said `fine` and `investigation` is being shown less than the system knew.

    No I/O, no DB writes. Returns `(severity: int, matched_terms: list[str])`.
